Privacy Policy for LexiFlow

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. LexiFlow is designed to help users learn and practice English. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to you when using LexiFlow.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the LexiFlow mobile application ("LexiFlow", "the App", "we", "us", or "our").

By using LexiFlow, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, LexiFlow may process or collect the following types of information.

2.1 Learning Data

LexiFlow may store information related to your learning activities, including:

  • Words and vocabulary you save
  • English phrases and sentence patterns
  • Grammar or language corrections
  • Learning sessions
  • Learning topics
  • Learning progress
  • Language preferences
  • English proficiency level
  • Saved learning content

This information is used to provide and improve the learning experience within LexiFlow.

2.2 Video and Media Information

LexiFlow may provide educational content associated with third-party video or media platforms.

The App may process information such as:

  • Video identifiers
  • Video titles
  • Channel or creator information
  • Thumbnail URLs
  • Video duration
  • Subtitle or caption information
  • Learning categories associated with videos

LexiFlow does not claim ownership of third-party videos or media. Such content remains subject to the terms and privacy policies of the respective content providers.

2.3 Speech and Microphone Access

Some LexiFlow features may allow you to practice English pronunciation or speaking using speech recognition.

When you choose to use these features, LexiFlow may request permission to access your device microphone.

Audio or speech data may be processed to:

  • Convert speech into text
  • Support pronunciation practice
  • Compare spoken words with learning content
  • Provide learning feedback

Microphone access is only used when required by a feature you choose to use and when permission has been granted.

2.4 Device and Technical Information

LexiFlow or third-party services integrated into the App may automatically collect limited technical information, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • Language and region settings
  • Diagnostic information
  • Crash information
  • Performance information

This information may be used to maintain application stability, diagnose errors, improve performance, and provide appropriate content.

2.5 Country and Region

LexiFlow may determine an approximate country or region based on information such as device settings, network information, or IP address.

This information may be used to provide appropriate language, learning content, and regional configuration.

LexiFlow does not require precise GPS location for its core English-learning functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by LexiFlow may be used to:

  • Provide English-learning features
  • Save and synchronize learning progress
  • Recommend relevant learning content
  • Provide vocabulary, subtitle, and pronunciation features
  • Personalize learning experiences
  • Improve application functionality
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • Prevent abuse and protect our services
  • Maintain the security and reliability of LexiFlow

4. Artificial Intelligence Features

LexiFlow may use artificial intelligence or automated processing to provide learning-related functionality.

These features may be used for purposes such as:

  • Language analysis
  • Translation
  • Vocabulary explanation
  • Grammar correction
  • Speech recognition
  • Learning recommendations
  • Subtitle processing
  • Generating educational feedback

Information submitted to an AI-powered feature may be processed by the service providers required to deliver that functionality. We aim to limit the information transmitted to what is necessary for the requested feature.

5. Translation Services

LexiFlow may use third-party translation services to translate educational content, subtitles, vocabulary, or other learning materials.

Text submitted for translation may be transmitted to the relevant translation provider for processing.

6. Third-Party Services

LexiFlow may use third-party services to provide certain application functionality.

These services may include:

  • Cloud infrastructure and hosting providers
  • Analytics and crash-reporting services
  • Authentication providers
  • Speech recognition services
  • Translation services
  • Artificial intelligence services
  • Video and media content providers

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

7. YouTube and Third-Party Video Content

LexiFlow may display, organize, reference, or provide access to educational videos hosted by third-party platforms such as YouTube.

LexiFlow does not own third-party video content. When you interact with third-party content, the respective provider's terms, policies, and privacy practices may apply.

YouTube content and services are subject to Google's Privacy Policy and YouTube's applicable Terms of Service.

8. Data Storage

Some LexiFlow data may be stored locally on your device. Certain features may also require information to be stored on secure backend or cloud infrastructure.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide LexiFlow's functionality, maintain our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature for which it is used.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate LexiFlow or provide a feature requested by the user.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request.

11. Advertising

LexiFlow may display advertising in certain versions of the App. Advertising providers may process limited device, advertising, interaction, or diagnostic information in accordance with applicable law and platform requirements.

Where required, LexiFlow will request appropriate permission before using information for tracking or personalized advertising.

12. App Tracking Transparency

On Apple platforms, if LexiFlow uses technologies that require tracking permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, the App will request permission before performing such tracking.

You may decline this permission. Declining tracking permission will not prevent you from accessing the core learning features of LexiFlow.

13. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to certain personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Withdraw certain permissions
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

Device permissions such as microphone access can also be managed through your device settings.

14. Account and Data Deletion

If LexiFlow provides user accounts, users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Where available, account deletion may be initiated directly within the App. You may also contact us using the contact information provided below.

Certain information may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or legitimate operational purposes.

15. Children's Privacy

LexiFlow is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable children's privacy laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through LexiFlow inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

16. International Data Processing

Some service providers used by LexiFlow may operate infrastructure in different countries. As a result, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required, we take reasonable measures to ensure that such processing is handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to LexiFlow, legal requirements, or our data practices.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.
