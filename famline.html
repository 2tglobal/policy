Privacy Policy

FamLine
Last updated: October 1, 2026

FamLine is designed to help families privately preserve and share memories, moments, and family information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FamLine ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use the FamLine application.

By using FamLine, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on how you use FamLine, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • Family member names or nicknames
  • Family relationships
  • Profile images or avatars
  • Photos and other family memories
  • Posts, moments, notes, captions, or descriptions
  • Emotions, reactions, or tags
  • Important family dates or events
  • Other information that you voluntarily add to the App

You control the information you add to FamLine.

3. Photos and Media

FamLine may request permission to access your photo library or camera when you choose to add photos or other media to your family timeline.

FamLine only accesses photos or media when required to provide a feature that you explicitly use.

We do not access your entire photo library for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

4. iCloud and CloudKit

FamLine may use Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services to store, synchronize, back up, and share data between your Apple devices and members of your family group.

Data stored using CloudKit may include:

  • Family member information
  • Family timeline content
  • Posts and memories
  • Photos and related metadata
  • Reactions and tags
  • Shared family information

Data stored in iCloud or CloudKit is managed using Apple's infrastructure and is also subject to Apple's privacy policies and iCloud terms.

FamLine does not receive your Apple ID password or iCloud password.

5. Family Sharing

FamLine may allow you to invite other people to participate in a shared family space using Apple's CloudKit sharing capabilities.

When you choose to share a family space, information contained in that shared space may become accessible to the family members you invite.

You should only invite people you trust and only upload information that you have permission to share.

Members of a shared family group may be able to view or interact with shared content depending on the permissions and features provided by the App.

6. How We Use Information

Information processed by FamLine is used to provide the application's core features, including:

  • Creating and displaying your family timeline
  • Managing family members
  • Saving family memories
  • Displaying posts, moments, emotions, and reactions
  • Synchronizing information between your devices
  • Sharing family information with invited family members
  • Maintaining the functionality and reliability of the App

7. Data We Do Not Sell

FamLine does not sell your personal information.

We do not sell your family information, photos, memories, or other content to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

8. Advertising and Tracking

FamLine does not use your private family content for advertising purposes.

FamLine does not use your photos, family relationships, posts, memories, or shared family information to create advertising profiles.

We do not track users across third-party applications or websites for advertising purposes unless this Privacy Policy is updated to explicitly describe such functionality.

9. Third-Party Services

FamLine may rely on services provided by Apple as part of the normal operation of the application, including:

  • Apple iCloud
  • Apple CloudKit
  • Apple Push Notification Service, where applicable
  • Apple App Store services

These services operate under Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

10. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through FamLine.

Where CloudKit is used, data is transmitted and stored using Apple's infrastructure and security mechanisms.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

Your information may remain stored on your device or in your iCloud account for as long as you keep the information within FamLine.

You may remove individual content from the App using the available deletion features.

Data shared with other family members may remain accessible to those members depending on Apple's CloudKit sharing behavior and the actions taken by members of the shared group.

12. Deleting Your Data

You can delete content that you have created in FamLine using the deletion features provided within the App.

You may also remove the App from your device.

Some synchronized data may continue to exist in your iCloud account until it is removed from iCloud or CloudKit according to Apple's data management mechanisms.

13. Children's Privacy

FamLine is designed for family use and may contain information about children that is added by parents, guardians, or other authorized family members.

Children should not independently provide personal information through FamLine without the permission and supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

Parents and guardians are responsible for ensuring that information about children is added and shared appropriately.

14. Your Privacy Choices

You can control certain permissions through your device settings, including access to:

  • Photos
  • Camera
  • Notifications
  • iCloud

Disabling certain permissions may prevent some FamLine features from functioning correctly.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to FamLine, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
