1. About KeepLater

KeepLater is designed to help users save and organize links to online content for convenient access later.

Depending on the available features, KeepLater may allow users to:

  • Save links shared from supported applications.
  • Paste a link manually into KeepLater.
  • Save information associated with a link, such as a title, thumbnail, description, creator name, or source.
  • Organize saved items using tags, favorites, collections, or topics.
  • Search and filter previously saved content.
  • Revisit supported online content later.
  • Open supported content using an appropriate playback or web-based experience.
Important:

KeepLater is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected with YouTube, TikTok, or other third-party content platforms unless explicitly stated otherwise.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, KeepLater may process the following information.

Saved Content Information

  • URLs submitted or shared by you
  • Video or content titles
  • Descriptions
  • Thumbnail URLs or images
  • Creator or channel names
  • Source platform
  • Content identifiers
  • Tags and collections created by you
  • Favorite status
  • Date and time an item was saved
  • Other metadata made publicly available by the source webpage or platform

User Preferences

KeepLater may store preferences such as:

  • Selected topics
  • Tags
  • Collections
  • Playback preferences
  • Language preferences
  • Application settings

Technical Information

Limited technical information may be processed where required for application operation, diagnostics, security, or crash analysis.

This information may include:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • Language and region
  • Crash information
  • Diagnostic information

3. Saved Links and Metadata

When you add an online link to KeepLater, the application may access that URL to obtain information required to display and organize the saved item.

Depending on the source, this may include publicly available information such as:

  • Title
  • Description
  • Thumbnail
  • Creator or channel
  • Content type
  • Publication information
  • Source website

KeepLater uses this information to create a convenient personal library of links selected by you.

KeepLater does not claim ownership of third-party content referenced by saved links.

4. Share Extension

KeepLater may provide a Share Extension that allows you to send a link from another application directly to KeepLater.

When you choose KeepLater from the system share sheet, the application may receive information that you intentionally share, including:

  • The shared URL
  • Shared text
  • Content title when available
  • Other information provided to the Share Extension by the source application or operating system

KeepLater does not use the Share Extension to access unrelated information from the source application.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed by KeepLater may be used to:

  • Save links requested by you
  • Retrieve metadata for saved links
  • Display your saved content library
  • Organize content using tags and collections
  • Provide search and filtering
  • Remember favorites and application preferences
  • Enable supported playback experiences
  • Improve application performance and stability
  • Diagnose errors and technical problems
  • Protect the application against abuse or security issues

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data Storage

KeepLater is designed to store application information locally on your device where possible.

Local information may include:

  • Saved URLs
  • Metadata
  • Tags
  • Collections
  • Favorites
  • Application preferences

Depending on future versions and enabled features, some information may also be processed through platform services or service providers when required to provide specific functionality.

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, misuse, or loss.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Third-Party Platforms and Services

KeepLater may interact with websites, content providers, or platforms when you intentionally save or view a link from those services.

Examples of supported or potentially supported sources may include:

  • YouTube
  • TikTok
  • Other websites or content platforms supported by KeepLater

These third-party services operate independently from KeepLater and may collect or process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

When content is displayed through an embedded webpage, web player, or similar third-party technology, the relevant provider may process information according to its own privacy practices.

KeepLater is not responsible for the privacy practices, availability, content, or policies of third-party services.

8. Device Permissions

KeepLater aims to request only permissions required for available features.

Network Access

Internet access is required to retrieve information from links, load thumbnails, access supported webpages, and display online content.

Share Extension

The system Share Extension allows KeepLater to receive links and content that you intentionally choose to share with the application.

Notifications

If notification features are available and you enable them, KeepLater may use notification permission to provide reminders or information requested by you.

Permissions can be managed or revoked through your device settings at any time.

9. Tracking and Advertising

KeepLater does not sell personal information.

KeepLater does not use saved URLs, tags, collections, or viewing organization data to create advertising profiles unless such a feature is explicitly introduced and disclosed in an updated Privacy Policy.

If advertising or analytics technologies are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated where required.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be disclosed only where necessary:

  • To protect application security
  • To investigate fraud, misuse, or abuse
  • To comply with applicable laws, court orders, or lawful requests
  • To protect our legal rights or the safety of users

11. Data Retention

Data stored locally by KeepLater generally remains on your device until you remove the relevant item, reset the application, or uninstall the application, subject to operating system backup behavior.

Any information processed by service providers, if applicable, may be retained only as necessary to provide the relevant service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect security.

12. Data Deletion

Where supported by the application, you can delete saved items, tags, collections, favorites, and related application data directly from KeepLater.

Uninstalling KeepLater generally removes locally stored application data, subject to operating system backup and restore functionality.

If you believe information relating to you is stored by a service controlled by us and would like to request deletion, please contact us using the email address below.

13. Children’s Privacy

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy
