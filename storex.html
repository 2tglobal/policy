Store-X Privacy Policy

Version 2026.10.11 · Effective 11 October 2026

English Tiếng Việt Customer Photo Cloud Backup Terms Account deletion
Summary: Store-X is a business-management application for stores and salons. Store-X processes account, store, synchronization, security, and support data to operate the service. Customer records and customer photos are entered by a store for its own business purposes. Customer-photo cloud backup is optional and is not enabled until an authorized store user gives an affirmative in-app authorization.

1. Who we are and where this policy applies

This policy applies to the Store-X applications for iOS and Android, Store-X APIs, and related web services. The developer or organization identified as the publisher of Store-X in the Apple App Store or Google Play is referred to in this policy as “Store-X,” “we,” “us,” or “our.” Store-X can be contacted at 2t.global921@gmail.com.

2. Our role and the store's role

Store-X determines how account administration, authentication, security, diagnostics, support, and service-operation data is processed. For customer records, appointments, notes, bills, customer photos, and other business content entered by a store, the store normally determines why the data is used and acts as the responsible business or data controller. Store-X processes that content on the store's instructions to provide synchronization, backup, recovery, and related functions.

A store is responsible for giving its customers any legally required notice, selecting an appropriate lawful basis, honoring customer requests, controlling staff access, and obtaining consent from the customer, parent, or guardian where applicable.

3. Information we collect or process

  • Account and sign-in data: name, email address, phone number where provided, account and provider identifiers, profile details returned by a selected sign-in provider, authentication tokens, store memberships, roles, and session information.
  • Store and business data: store name, address, selected map coordinates, contact details, settings, staff, services, appointments, bills, expenses, notes, and related synchronization metadata.
  • Customer records: customer name, phone number, notes, appointments, transaction or service history, and other details an authorized store user chooses to enter or import.
  • Contacts selected or imported by a user: the app may read contact names, phone numbers, and available contact thumbnails to present an import feature. Only records the user chooses to save as Store-X customers are submitted for Store-X synchronization.
  • Photos and media: store images and customer images selected through the system photo picker or captured with the camera. Customer photos are uploaded to cloud backup only after the current backup authorization is accepted.
  • Location: an approximate or precise device location when the user grants permission and requests location-based store setup or map functions. Store-X may save the location selected for a store.
  • Device and notification data: device identifier, device name, app and operating-system version, language, push-notification token, notification preferences, network and session information.
  • Usage, diagnostics, and security data: feature events, timestamps, crash reports, error logs, IP address and request metadata, abuse-prevention signals, and performance information.
  • Support communications: messages, attachments, and contact information submitted when requesting help, privacy support, or deletion.

We receive information directly from users and stores, from the device when permission or a feature requires it, from sign-in providers selected by the user, and automatically when the app communicates with Store-X services.

4. Device permissions and user choice

  • Camera and photos: used only when a user chooses to capture or select an image for a Store-X feature.
  • Contacts: used to display the optional customer-import experience. Permission can be denied or revoked in device settings.
  • Location: used for optional store-location and map features while the app is in use. Permission can be denied or revoked in device settings.
  • Notifications: used for reminders, appointments, and service notices when enabled.

Denying an optional permission may disable the related feature but does not prevent use of unrelated Store-X functions.

5. Why we process information

We process information to create and administer accounts; authenticate users; provide store-management, synchronization, backup, recovery, notification, and support functions; personalize language and settings; diagnose failures; measure and improve service performance; secure accounts; prevent fraud and abuse; enforce agreements; and comply with legal obligations.

Depending on the data and applicable law, processing may be based on performance of a contract, the store's documented instructions, legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, compliance with law, or consent. Where consent is the legal basis, it may be withdrawn for future processing, without affecting processing already carried out lawfully.

6. Customer-photo cloud backup

Cloud backup is off until an authorized store user accepts the current Customer Photo Cloud Backup Terms. After authorization, eligible customer photos already stored by that store and new customer photos may be uploaded in the foreground or background for synchronization, recovery, and access by authorized users of the signed-in store.

Customer photos are stored using Cloudflare R2. Time-limited upload or download links may be used. Store-X does not use customer photos for advertising, facial recognition, biometric identification, surveillance, sale of personal information, or training general-purpose artificial-intelligence models.

7. Service providers and disclosures

Store-X may provide limited data to service providers that support hosting, database operations, object storage, authentication, notifications, analytics, crash reporting, security, communications, and support. These may include Cloudflare for customer-photo storage; Google or Firebase for authentication, notifications, analytics, and crash reporting; and Apple, Google, or Facebook when a user chooses the relevant sign-in method.

Service providers may process data only for the services they provide, under their applicable terms and safeguards. We require providers handling Store-X personal data to apply protections consistent with this policy and applicable law. We may also disclose information to professional advisers under confidentiality duties, in a business reorganization subject to appropriate safeguards, to protect users or the service, or when a valid legal request requires it.

8. No sale, targeted advertising, or biometric use

Store-X does not sell customer photos or customer records and does not share them for cross-context behavioral advertising. Store-X does not create facial templates or use customer photos to identify a person. If these practices materially change, we will update this policy and provide any notice and choice required by law.

9. International processing

Store-X and its providers may process data outside the country where a store, user, or customer is located. Where required, we use recognized safeguards such as contractual protections and provider data-protection terms. Storage location depends on production configuration and provider infrastructure; Store-X does not promise that all data remains in one country.

10. Retention

Account, store, and synchronized business data is retained while the relevant account or store uses the service and thereafter only as reasonably necessary for deletion processing, security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and enforcement. Authentication records and push tokens are retained until they expire, are revoked, or are no longer needed. Diagnostics and server logs are kept only for periods reasonably necessary for reliability and security.

Active cloud photos remain until an authorized store user deletes them, the store closes the relevant service relationship, or a verified deletion request is completed, subject to lawful exceptions. Deletion removes the active R2 object and marks the related service record as deleted. Limited copies may remain temporarily in protected logs or disaster-recovery backups until overwritten or expired under the applicable backup cycle.

11. Account deletion, photo deletion, and withdrawal

An account holder may request deletion of a Store-X account and associated personal data through an available in-app account-deletion control or by emailing 2t.global921@gmail.com with the subject “Store-X Account Deletion Request.” Include the sign-in email and store name, but do not send passwords or authentication tokens. We may verify identity and authority before acting.

After verification, Store-X will delete or anonymize data associated with the account unless it must be retained for security, fraud prevention, financial or tax records, legal claims, or another legal obligation. Where a record belongs to a store with multiple authorized users, Store-X may need authorization from the store owner before deleting shared business data.

Stores can delete individual customer photos using available Store-X deletion actions or submit a verified request. Withdrawing authorization for future photo backup does not automatically delete photos already uploaded; those photos must be deleted separately. A customer whose information was entered by a store should normally contact that store first. Store-X will assist with verified requests as required.

12. Privacy rights

Depending on applicable law and the relationship to Store-X or a store, individuals may have rights to know or access their data; correct inaccurate data; request deletion; restrict or object to processing; receive a portable copy; withdraw consent; and complain to a data-protection authority. Where applicable, individuals may also appeal a denied request and may exercise rights without unlawful discrimination.

Submit a request to 2t.global921@gmail.com. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify identity, authority, and the applicable store. Authorized agents may be required to provide proof of authorization.

13. Security

Store-X uses controls designed for the nature of the service, including encrypted network transport, authenticated sessions, tenant and store scoping, time-limited object access, integrity checks for media uploads, rate limiting, logging, and access controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Stores must protect their devices and credentials, review staff access, keep applications updated, and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

14. Children

Store-X is a business application and is not directed to children. A store must not create records about or upload photos of a child unless it has authority, a valid lawful basis, and any parent or guardian permission required by applicable law. Store-X does not knowingly invite children to create Store-X business accounts.

15. Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect product, provider, or legal changes. The current version and effective date will be posted here. Material changes affecting customer-photo backup may require renewed in-app authorization before additional photos are uploaded.

Privacy and deletion contact: 2t.global921@gmail.com

Store-X quyết định cách xử lý dữ liệu phục vụ quản trị tài khoản, xác thực, bảo mật, chẩn đoán, hỗ trợ và vận hành dịch vụ. Đối với hồ sơ khách hàng, lịch hẹn, ghi chú, hóa đơn, ảnh khách hàng và nội dung nghiệp vụ do cửa hàng nhập, cửa hàng thông thường quyết định mục đích sử dụng và là đơn vị kinh doanh hoặc bên kiểm soát dữ liệu chịu trách nhiệm. Store-X xử lý nội dung đó theo chỉ dẫn của cửa hàng để cung cấp đồng bộ, sao lưu, khôi phục và các chức năng liên quan.

Cửa hàng chịu trách nhiệm thông báo cho khách hàng khi pháp luật yêu cầu, lựa chọn căn cứ xử lý hợp pháp, giải quyết yêu cầu của khách hàng, quản lý quyền nhân viên và xin sự đồng ý của khách hàng, cha mẹ hoặc người giám hộ khi áp dụng.

  • Trao đổi hỗ trợ: nội dung, tệp đính kèm và thông tin liên hệ gửi khi yêu cầu hỗ trợ, quyền riêng tư hoặc xóa dữ liệu.

Thông tin được nhận trực tiếp từ người dùng và cửa hàng, từ thiết bị khi tính năng hoặc quyền yêu cầu, từ nhà cung cấp đăng nhập do người dùng chọn, và tự động khi ứng dụng giao tiếp với dịch vụ Store-X.
